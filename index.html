Traditional Language

Flyingo Bisdomic

The old language of Nomashae, shaped by air, motion, and balance.

"Modrn dei es da best dei for skai surf. Nex, mi had un pom an smol chok bar."

"Modern day is the best day for sky surfing. Next, I had an apple and a small chocolate bar."

About Nomashae

Est. 1 September 2025

Founded in 2025, Nomashae is a democratic micronation inspired by the nomadic spirit and energy of Avatar: The Last Airbender. Built on the philosophy of balance, peace, and harmony across all four elements.
